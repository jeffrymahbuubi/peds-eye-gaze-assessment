# Vesa Mount Assembly Instructions

The VESA Mount allows you to mount a Gazepoint eye tracker directly to your computer monitor.

You can either use the VESA mounting holes on the back of a monitor or the 3M Adhesive strips (provided) to attach the bracket to your monitor.

**Vesa Mount Kit includes:**

- VESA Rods (2)

- VESA Arms (2)

- Black M4 Thumbscrews (6)

- 6-32 1/2" Washer Screws (4)

- 3M Adhesive Strips (4)

* Assembly requires #2 phillips screwdriver

We recommend that you first install the Gazepoint installer software before mounting the eye tracker.

[FIGURE]

1. Attach VESA Arms to VESA Rods using 6/32 1/2" Washer Screws. These are pre-threaded to the VESA Rods and will have to be removed. For 100mm VESA Mount holes, ensure that the VESA Rods are oriented so that the lower bends are away from the centerline and the VESA Arms bend towards the centerline, as shown in the diagram.

5. Align with VESA holes and use 4 Black M4 Thumbscrews to affix into place. If VESA holes are not available on your monitor, affix 3M Adhesive Strips to the VESA Rods using two for each rod and mount the VESA Rods to the back of your monitor with the adhesive strip.

6. Plug in the USB power and data cables to your computer.

[FIGURE]

7. Position yourself so that the monitor/eye tracker is 65cm (25") away and 40cm (15") below your eye level. Angle the eye tracker so that it points upwards towards your face.

Gazepoint VESA Mount setup is now complete.
