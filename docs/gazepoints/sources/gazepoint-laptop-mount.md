# Laptop Mount Assembly Instructions

The Laptop Mount allows you to use a Gazepoint eye tracker with a laptop.

Laptop Mount Kit includes:

- Laptop Mount

- Black M4 Thumbscrews (2)

We recommend that you first install the Gazepoint installer software before mounting the eye tracker.

[FIGURE]

1. Position the Laptop Mount so that the flared ends are to the back of the eye tracker. Push the eye tracker mount tabs into the slots of the Laptop Mount and thread the M4 Thumbscrews to tighten.

[FIGURE]

2. Connect the USB and power cables to the eye tracker and place on the keyboard as close to the lower edge of the screen as possible and angle upwards to approximately 45° towards your face and tighten M4 Thumbscrews.
